# ADR 013 - Lead capture and CTA architecture

**Status:** Decided · 2026-11-05 · **Decision authority:** owner instruction "lead generation is critical; the CTA must be embedded into the build" (2026-11-05); ratification tracked in `HANDOFF.md`.
**Needs:** owner review of partner model and lead pricing (U18) - not a blocker.

**Decides:** how CTAs, lead forms, consent, routing, retention, and funnel measurement are built.

## Context

- Lead generation is a primary business goal: the sites earn attention, and qualified inquiries are routed to partner professionals per industry.
- The reference site (`accidentinjurynews.com`) is itself a lead-gen property: persistent contact CTA, "Consult Lawyers", attorney-advertising disclaimers.
- Leads are PII; the domain has legal, financial, and health channels with advertising/advice constraints.
- The build must make the CTA structural, not a footer afterthought, and keep it measurable without dark patterns.

## Decision

### 1. Product surface

- **One primary CTA per page**: channel home hero + sticky mobile bar, after-summary and end-of-article cards, compact blocks on search/about, quiet footer link. Placement is channel config (JSONB), not hardcoded JSX.
- **L1 offer:** "Request a consultation" / "Talk to a specialist" → short form (name, email, phone optional, need, consent).
- **L2 offer:** "Get the weekly brief" → email only, double opt-in.
- CTA copy and disclaimers are content per channel/locale; design intent in `.work.ui/design-system/20261105-CTA-BRIEF.md`, business case in `.work.biz/strategy/lead-generation.md`.

### 2. API

- `POST /v1/leads` (public, no auth): body `{industry, kind: consultation|brief, name, email, phone?, company?, message?, locale, consent, source_page?, article_id?, utm?}`.
  - `201` with `{id}` only (no PII echo).
  - `422` validation; `429` rate limit (5/hour/IP) with `Retry-After`; honeypot + duplicate suppression (10 min).
  - Consent must be `true`; the current consent text version is stored.
- Admin: `GET /v1/admin/leads` (filter industry/status), `PATCH /v1/admin/leads/{id}` (status), `POST /v1/admin/leads/{id}/assign`, `GET|POST|PATCH /v1/admin/partners`, `GET /v1/admin/leads/{id}/export`, `DELETE /v1/admin/leads/{id}` (DSAR).
- No public endpoint lists, searches, or returns lead PII.

### 3. Data model

| Table | Columns | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `partners` | `id`, `industry_id`, `name`, `contact_email`, `status`, `weight`, `regions` JSONB, `created_at` | One active partner per channel at v1 |
| `leads` | `id`, `industry_id`, `partner_id`, `kind`, `name`, `email`, `phone`, `company`, `message`, `locale`, `source_page`, `article_id`, `utm` JSONB, `consent_version`, `consent_at`, `ip_hash`, `ua_hash`, `status`, `created_at`, `updated_at` | `status ∈ {new, routed, contacted, qualified, closed, spam}` |
| `lead_events` | `id`, `lead_id`, `actor`, `action`, `payload` JSONB, `created_at` | Append-only audit |
| `cta_events` | `id`, `industry_id`, `event`, `placement`, `article_id`, `locale`, `created_at`, `session_hash` | No PII; 90-day retention |

- `ip_hash`/`ua_hash` are SHA-256 with a server pepper, never raw.
- Lead retention 24 months; spam/rejected 90 days; deletion on request within 30 days.

### 4. Routing

- Auto-route by `industry_id` to the single active partner; unmatched → admin queue.
- Partner notification by email (transactional) + admin inbox; acknowledgment SLA 24 h, tracked in `leads.status`.
- No cross-channel visibility; no partner API in v1.
- Every status change writes `lead_events` and an `audit_events` row when actor is admin.

### 5. Consent, privacy, and anti-abuse

- Explicit unchecked consent, versioned text, timestamp; privacy link beside the field.
- Forms warn against sensitive data; server rejects obvious PHI/account-number/SSN patterns in `message`.
- Honeypot field + 5/hour/IP + duplicate suppression; no CAPTCHA in v1.
- No third-party marketing pixels without owner approval (U9).
- DSAR export/erase via admin API; retention job purges expired records.

### 6. Instrumentation

- Client events: `cta_impression`, `cta_click`, `lead_form_start`, `lead_submit{result}` - no PII.
- Server: `leads_total{industry,status}`, `leads_rate_limited_total`, `lead_route_latency_seconds`, funnel conversion dashboard.
- Qualification is a manual admin transition (no automated scoring in v1).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** lead-gen is a first-class, measurable surface; consent/privacy and per-channel ethics are enforced centrally; partner routing is auditable; no dark patterns.
- **Negative:** more PII surface (mitigated by classification, hashing, retention); one-partner-per-channel limits coverage at launch; manual qualification adds operator work.
- **Neutral:** automated scoring/geo-routing and partner API are Phase B.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why rejected |
|--------|--------------|
| Third-party lead-gen embed | Data ownership, cost, no control over consent/UX |
| Phone-only CTA | Excludes readers, unmeasurable, poor a11y |
| Exit-intent popups | Dark pattern; contradicts voice and brand |
| Gated articles | Damages the news product and SEO |
| Store leads in a CRM directly | Premature integration; v1 needs the inbox + email |
